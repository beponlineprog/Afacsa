About Us

Who We Are

Apostolic Faith Assembly of Africa is a Christ-centred, Bible-believing Christian church devoted to sharing the good news of Jesus Christ and helping people grow in a personal, living relationship with God. We believe the Bible is the foundation for faith, Christian character, worship, and everyday living, and we seek to follow the teaching and example of Jesus Christ in all that we do.


We are a spiritual family made up of people from different backgrounds who come together to worship God, pray, learn His Word, encourage one another, and serve our communities. We believe the church is called not only to gather for worship, but also to demonstrate God's love through compassion, integrity, forgiveness, unity, and practical service. Through fellowship, discipleship, prayer, and the work of the Holy Spirit, we aim to help believers mature in faith and use their gifts to build up the body of Christ.


Our Vision

Our vision is to build a strong, Christ-centred and Spirit-led church that transforms lives through the Word of God, prayer, and the power of the Holy Spirit. We desire to see individuals and families strengthened in faith, believers equipped to serve, leaders raised with godly character, and communities touched by the love and truth of Jesus Christ.


We aspire to be a church where people can discover hope, grow spiritually, develop their God-given gifts, and become faithful disciples of Jesus Christ. Our ultimate desire is to glorify God in everything and prepare people to live according to His will and the hope of His Kingdom.


Our Mission

Our mission is to fulfil the calling of the church by sharing the Gospel, nurturing believers, equipping Christian leaders, strengthening families, and serving people with the love of Christ. We seek to put this mission into practice through the following commitments:


  • Preach the Gospel: Share the message of salvation through Jesus Christ with love, clarity, and faithfulness to Scripture.
  • Make Disciples: Teach the Word of God and encourage believers to grow in prayer, worship, obedience, and Christlike character.
  • Build a Life of Prayer: Encourage personal and corporate prayer, trusting God for wisdom, guidance, strength, and spiritual growth.
  • Develop Leaders: Identify, mentor, and equip faithful servants to lead with humility, integrity, accountability, and compassion.
  • Strengthen Families: Promote love, respect, forgiveness, godly values, and healthy relationships within families and the wider church community.
  • Serve Our Communities: Demonstrate the love of Christ through kindness, practical support, encouragement, and care for people in need.
  • Promote Unity and Fellowship: Build a welcoming church community where believers support one another and work together in peace and love.
  • Live as Witnesses of Christ: Encourage every member to reflect Jesus through honest conduct, good works, and faithful service in daily life.
